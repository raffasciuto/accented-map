Your voice matters here — but only for as long as this project exists.

Recordings are stored securely, used only for this thesis, and permanently deleted afterward. Nothing is sold, shared, or used to train any external model.

Recordings are uploaded to a private Firebase Storage bucket and never made public beyond this map. Each memo is tied to a randomly generated username, not to your identity — no account, no email, no device fingerprint is collected.

Location is stored only as the approximate coordinates you consented to share when recording, used solely to place the dot on the map.

The transcript and audio are sent once to language-analysis models to produce the language and classification results shown in your memo card. They are not retained by those services for training.

When this thesis project concludes, the entire database and storage bucket are deleted in full.
