What this isn't measuring

Accent classification only exists as a trained model for four languages — English (87% accuracy, 16 accent categories), German (75.5%, 4 categories), Spanish (68.5%, 6 categories), Italian (accuracy never published, 5 categories). Every other language is forced through the English model anyway rather than hidden behind a "not supported" message — the wrong, overconfident answer that produces is the point, not a bug being tolerated.

Emotion recognition (emotion2vec+) has no published accuracy figure at all, and like nearly every speech-emotion model, was trained overwhelmingly on English-language data.

The "acoustic features" shown elsewhere in this app (energy, tension, expressiveness) are raw waveform measurements — volume and pitch variance — not emotions. Nothing in this project can tell you how someone feels.
