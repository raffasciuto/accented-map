Did you know that AI is trained on 94% english data?

Since its first developments back in the 80s, AI has been mostly trained on elgish. Defaulting to english means that it was also trained on a certain perspective of the word - those wo can speak it fluently.

Even Spanish, one of the words most speaken languages, is only supported in 20% of the dataset - creating a huge gap in halluzinations when communicating on this language. This creates a bigger acces barrier in a system that lives on the marketing of "everyone can make everything"

Language has been the only way to communicate stories and heritage in communities. The bigger the gap, the more only one perspective and vision of the word is perpetuated, making us all think the same. A pattern, that we already see online thorugh the mass opinion shifting in social media.
